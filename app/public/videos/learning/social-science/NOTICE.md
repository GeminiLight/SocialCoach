# SocialCoach social science films

Original animation and editorial production for SocialCoach. H is a synthetic
narration voice, generated with Breeze TTS 2 by BreezeBlue. Model weights and
reference audio are not distributed here.

Upstream model and license: https://huggingface.co/BreezeBlue/Breeze-TTS-2

The application includes source citations, timed transcripts, and the distinction
between research findings, theoretical arguments, model illustrations, and fiction.
The repository's code license does not grant rights to third-party model materials.
